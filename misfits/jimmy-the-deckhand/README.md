# Jimmy the Deckhand

![jimmy-the-deckhand](../../assets/jimmy-the-deckhand.png)

## Ahoy, Mateys!

Welcome aboard to Jimmy the Deckhand - the most reliable crew member in your fleet of AWS services!

## What's a Deckhand?

On a sailing ship, the deckhand is the trusty crew member who handles all the essential day-to-day tasks that keep the vessel shipshape and sailing smoothly. They're not the captain making the big decisions, but without them, the ship would quickly run aground!

Jimmy the Deckhand serves the same purpose for your cloud applications - a collection of utilities and helpers that handle the mundane but critical tasks so your flagship services can focus on their main mission.

## What's in Jimmy's Toolbox?

Just as a deckhand carries a variety of tools for maintaining the ship, Jimmy comes equipped with a collection of utilities to help your application navigate the treacherous waters of cloud development:

- **Date and Time Utilities**: Like a ship's chronometer, these tools help you keep precise time across your application
- **Request Handlers**: Similar to a lookout in the crow's nest, these utilities spot and process incoming requests
- **Type Definitions**: As essential as a ship's charts, these provide structure and safety to your codebase
- **Monitoring Tools**: Acting as the ship's log, these help you track your application's journey

## Why "Jimmy the Deckhand"?

In the grand fleet of "The Band of Misfits," each service is named after a colorful character you might find aboard a pirate ship. While Jack in the Cloud might be swinging from the rigging to deliver your scheduled events, Jimmy is below deck, making sure all the small but essential tasks are handled properly.

## Getting Started

To add Jimmy to your crew, simply install the package:

```bash
pnpm add @the-band-of-misfits/jimmy-the-deckhand-utils
```

Then import the utilities you need:

```typescript
import { ISO8601 } from '@the-band-of-misfits/jimmy-the-deckhand-utils';
```
